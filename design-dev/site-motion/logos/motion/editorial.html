Built in our Minneapolis studio. All of it.

Bent, machined, wired, coated and packed under one roof in Roseville, north of Minneapolis. The powder coat booth is ours, so a color is never “close enough” and a fixture never waits on a subcontractor.

Solid brass, high-gauge steel and aluminum, hand-blown opal glass. UL listed. Warrantied by the people who built it.

  • Fourteen powder coats, mixed and sprayed in our own booth.
  • One roof, from raw brass to the box on your porch.
  • Weeks from order to ship, because nothing sits in a warehouse.
