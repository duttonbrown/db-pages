About windows: Engagement metrics use rolling windows by design. The 90-day Active Rate is the most responsive — use it for monthly health checks. The 12-month Active Account Rate (in the Quarterly Strategic panel at the bottom) is heavily smoothed and best read year-over-year, not month-to-month.

90-Day Active Rate % of lifetime trade-tagged accounts that placed an order in the trailing 90 days. Sensitive to monthly change — use this for ongoing program health.

Repeat rate % of trade customers with more than one order in the trailing 12 months. A 12-month rolling metric — moves slowly by design.

Quarterly strategic view: 12-month Active Account Rate % of lifetime trade-tagged accounts active in trailing 12 months. Read this YoY, not MoM — a 1-pt change here represents real movement.
